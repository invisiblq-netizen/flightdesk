      Import a flight plan to select the aircraft profile
      PF and PM flows are shown side by side. Only your selected PF or PM actions are editable; CM actions are shared crew acknowledgements for this two-pilot app. Each phase contains sections and role-tagged actions. Phases unlock in order and advance when every PF, PM and shared CM action in the current phase is complete. Items marked AUTO can be checked from Fenix telemetry for your role in the current unlocked phase. This is a simulator scan-flow aid, not an operational checklist; follow the exact aircraft/add-on FCOM, QRH and applicable SOP.
